Privacy Policy for YTM Manager

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how YTM Manager ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our web application located at https://ytm.yapa24.netcraze.pro/.

1. Information We Collect

When you log in using Google OAuth 2.0, we request access to the following data:

  • Basic Profile Information: Your email address, name, and profile picture. This is used to identify you within the application and display your profile.
  • YouTube Data (Read-Only): We request the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly scope. This allows us to read metadata (such as video titles, descriptions, channel names, and durations) for the specific video links you choose to save and manage within our application.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively to provide and improve the YTM Manager service. Specifically:

  • To authenticate you and maintain your session.
  • To fetch and display metadata for the video links you add to your personal library.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, or profiling purposes.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your Google OAuth refresh tokens are stored securely on our private server. We do not store your Google account password. Access tokens are kept in memory and are only used to communicate with the YouTube API on your behalf.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

You can revoke our access to your Google account and YouTube data at any time by visiting the Google Security Settings page. Once access is revoked, we immediately delete your stored refresh tokens. You may also request the deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us.
